# Deferred scope

This register records unsupported capabilities, the needs they would serve, and the evidence required to reconsider them. [Delivery](../plans/delivery.md) owns implementation sequence; [acceptance](../design/acceptance.md) owns intended financial behavior.

**Later planned** capabilities have a consuming increment in delivery. **Deferred** capabilities require the stated demand or evidence before implementation is planned. Neither status makes their detailed representations prerequisites for earlier work.

| Capability and status | Need | Supported limitation and revisit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interpreter qualification and interpretation comparison — **Deferred** | A new parser, rule interpreter or model can restate retained history even when correction guards remain valid. Strict replay of old software does not establish that the new interpretation is acceptable. | No qualification or interpretation-comparison workflow is implemented. Revisit when old/new interpreter sets can consume identical retained inputs and produce usable comparisons. Qualification remains a separate [release-readiness gate](../plans/delivery.md#interpreter-qualification-separate-release-readiness-gate) before presenting external importers as public and stable. |
| Recurring fixed-amount rule splits — **Deferred** | A recurring payment may need several conserving allocations rather than one category. | Conserving reviewed splits are supported; standing rule splits are not. Revisit for an actual recurring-policy consumer, with stable child keys and exact conservation. A changed or non-conserving bill must not silently fall back. |
| Match views for unimplemented processors — **Deferred to their consumers** | Typed matching needs meaningful pre-enrichment facts from the processor that owns them. | Bank/card matching and permitted assignments are implemented. Add other match views with their processors and independently checked expectations. |
| Importer authoring template and testkit — **Deferred until demanded** | Third-party authors may need a reliable way to prove extraction fidelity and protocol conformance. | The external acceptance package proves installed loading, not a general authoring framework. Revisit with actual importer authors and recurring needs; interface stability remains subject to qualification. |
| Further formats, OCR, hledger, beangulp compatibility, legacy adoption and suggestion tools — **Deferred demand-driven breadth** | Different evidence formats and adoption workflows can make the product useful without changing economic truth. | Revisit for a concrete user workflow through existing boundaries, with explicit fidelity limits and no automatic network access. |
| Inventory/corporate actions, contract/on-chain processors, reference analysis/consolidation, valuation/FX/performance and jurisdiction models — **Later planned, delivery steps 3–6** | These require distinct economic calculations, lineage, authority and missing-data policies beyond a bank/card quantity record. | Foundational schemas and independently calculated fixtures establish contracts, not runtime support. Implement calculations and processor-specific decisions with their consumers in [delivery](../plans/delivery.md). |
| OFX semantics not established by the supported profile — **Deferred format evidence** | Available funds/credit, foreign transaction amounts and statement-closing messages must not be mistaken for known model balances or invented original amounts. | The [OFX profile](./ofx-profile.md) owns accepted evidence and unsupported semantics. Extend it only with verified format semantics and a consuming fixture. |
